About prayer times in Johor Bahru

Prayer times in Johor Bahru, Malaysia, shift a little every day as the sun's path changes through the year. Muslims here observe five daily prayers — Fajr before dawn, Dhuhr after midday, Asr in the afternoon, Maghrib just after sunset, and Isha at night — and this page gives the exact time of each, for every day of 2026. SalahTime computes them for Johor Bahru (1.4927, 103.7414) with precise solar-position mathematics, following the Jabatan Kemajuan Islam Malaysia (JAKIM) convention used in Malaysia. The “Today” card above always shows the current day's times, calculated live in your browser.

Prayer times here follow the JAKIM convention (Fajr 20°, Isha 18°) set by Malaysia's Department of Islamic Development — the official standard used by mosques throughout Johor Bahru.

Johor Bahru keeps Asia/Kuala Lumpur time (UTC+08:00), with no daylight-saving changes in 2026. SalahTime computes against the IANA timezone database, so any clock change is applied automatically — the times here always match the local clock in Johor Bahru.
