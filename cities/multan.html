About prayer times in Multan

Prayer times in Multan, Pakistan, shift a little every day as the sun's path changes through the year. Muslims here observe five daily prayers — Fajr before dawn, Dhuhr after midday, Asr in the afternoon, Maghrib just after sunset, and Isha at night — and this page gives the exact time of each, for every day of 2026. SalahTime computes them for Multan (30.1575, 71.5249) with precise solar-position mathematics, following the University of Islamic Sciences, Karachi convention used in Pakistan. The “Today” card above always shows the current day's times, calculated live in your browser.

Times here follow the University of Islamic Sciences, Karachi convention (Fajr 18°, Isha 18°) — the long-established standard for Pakistan and the wider subcontinent, used by the vast majority of printed timetables in Multan.

Multan keeps Asia/Karachi time (UTC+05:00), with no daylight-saving changes in 2026. SalahTime computes against the IANA timezone database, so any clock change is applied automatically — the times here always match the local clock in Multan.

Frequently asked questions

What calculation method is used for prayer times in Multan?

SalahTime uses the University of Islamic Sciences, Karachi — Fajr 18°, Isha 18°. This is the standard convention in Pakistan — compare it with the other ten methods using the selector above the timetable.

What time is Fajr today in Multan?

Fajr shifts a minute or two each day; the exact time is in the “Today” card at the top of this page, computed live for the current date. Imsak (the start of the fast) falls about ten minutes before Fajr.

How are the prayer times on this page calculated?
