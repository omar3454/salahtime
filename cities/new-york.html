About prayer times in New York

Prayer times in New York, United States, shift a little every day as the sun's path changes through the year. Muslims here observe five daily prayers — Fajr before dawn, Dhuhr after midday, Asr in the afternoon, Maghrib just after sunset, and Isha at night — and this page gives the exact time of each, for every day of 2026. SalahTime computes them for New York (40.7128, -74.006) with precise solar-position mathematics, following the Islamic Society of North America convention used in United States. The “Today” card above always shows the current day's times, calculated live in your browser.

Times here follow the Islamic Society of North America convention (Fajr 15°, Isha 15°) — the standard reference for United States, used by most mosques and Islamic centers in New York.

New York keeps America/New York time (UTC−05:00) and observes daylight saving in 2026, shifting to UTC−04:00 in the warmer months. SalahTime computes against the IANA timezone database, so any clock change is applied automatically — the times here always match the local clock in New York.

Frequently asked questions

What calculation method is used for prayer times in New York?

SalahTime uses the Islamic Society of North America — Fajr 15°, Isha 15°. This is the standard convention in United States — compare it with the other ten methods using the selector above the timetable.

What time is Fajr today in New York?

Fajr shifts a minute or two each day; the exact time is in the “Today” card at the top of this page, computed live for the current date. Imsak (the start of the fast) falls about ten minutes before Fajr.

How are the prayer times on this page calculated?

From the sun's declination and the equation of time at New York's coordinates, applying the twilight angles of the chosen method (Fajr 15°, Isha at 15°). The engine has been verified against the Aladhan prayer-times API across multiple cities and dates.

Can I switch the calculation method?

Yes — the dropdown above the timetable switches between all eleven major methods, with a Standard/Hanafi Asr toggle. The timetable and the “Today” card recompute instantly in your browser.
