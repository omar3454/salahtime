About prayer times in Toronto

Prayer times in Toronto, Canada, shift a little every day as the sun's path changes through the year. Muslims here observe five daily prayers — Fajr before dawn, Dhuhr after midday, Asr in the afternoon, Maghrib just after sunset, and Isha at night — and this page gives the exact time of each, for every day of 2026. SalahTime computes them for Toronto (43.6532, -79.3832) with precise solar-position mathematics, following the Islamic Society of North America convention used in Canada. The “Today” card above always shows the current day's times, calculated live in your browser.

Times here follow the Islamic Society of North America convention (Fajr 15°, Isha 15°) — the standard reference for Canada, used by most mosques and Islamic centers in Toronto.

Toronto keeps America/Toronto time (UTC−05:00) and observes daylight saving in 2026, shifting to UTC−04:00 in the warmer months. SalahTime computes against the IANA timezone database, so any clock change is applied automatically — the times here always match the local clock in Toronto.

Frequently asked questions

What calculation method is used for prayer times in Toronto?

SalahTime uses the Islamic Society of North America — Fajr 15°, Isha 15°. This is the standard convention in Canada — compare it with the other ten methods using the selector above the timetable.

What time is Fajr today in Toronto?

Fajr shifts a minute or two each day; the exact time is in the “Today” card at the top of this page, computed live for the current date. Imsak (the start of the fast) falls about ten minutes before Fajr.

How are the prayer times on this page calculated?

From the sun's declination and the equation of time at Toronto's coordinates, applying the twilight angles of the chosen method (Fajr 15°, Isha at 15°). The engine has been verified against the Aladhan prayer-times API across multiple cities and dates.
