About prayer times in Izmir

Prayer times in Izmir, Turkey, shift a little every day as the sun's path changes through the year. Muslims here observe five daily prayers — Fajr before dawn, Dhuhr after midday, Asr in the afternoon, Maghrib just after sunset, and Isha at night — and this page gives the exact time of each, for every day of 2026. SalahTime computes them for Izmir (38.4192, 27.1287) with precise solar-position mathematics, following the Diyanet, Turkey convention used in Turkey. The “Today” card above always shows the current day's times, calculated live in your browser.

This page uses the Diyanet convention of Turkey's Directorate of Religious Affairs (Fajr 18°, Isha 17°, with small precautionary minute adjustments) — the official standard behind every mosque timetable in Izmir.

Izmir keeps Europe/Istanbul time (UTC+03:00), with no daylight-saving changes in 2026. SalahTime computes against the IANA timezone database, so any clock change is applied automatically — the times here always match the local clock in Izmir.

Frequently asked questions

What calculation method is used for prayer times in Izmir?

SalahTime uses the Diyanet, Turkey — Fajr 18°, Isha 17°. This is the standard convention in Turkey — compare it with the other ten methods using the selector above the timetable.

What time is Fajr today in Izmir?

Fajr shifts a minute or two each day; the exact time is in the “Today” card at the top of this page, computed live for the current date. Imsak (the start of the fast) falls about ten minutes before Fajr.

How are the prayer times on this page calculated?

From the sun's declination and the equation of time at Izmir's coordinates, applying the twilight angles of the chosen method (Fajr 18°, Isha at 17°). The engine has been verified against the Aladhan prayer-times API across multiple cities and dates.

Can I switch the calculation method?

Yes — the dropdown above the timetable switches between all eleven major methods, with a Standard/Hanafi Asr toggle. The timetable and the “Today” card recompute instantly in your browser.
