About prayer times in Lahore

Prayer times in Lahore, Pakistan, shift a little every day as the sun's path changes through the year. Muslims here observe five daily prayers — Fajr before dawn, Dhuhr after midday, Asr in the afternoon, Maghrib just after sunset, and Isha at night — and this page gives the exact time of each, for every day of 2026. SalahTime computes them for Lahore (31.5204, 74.3587) with precise solar-position mathematics, following the University of Islamic Sciences, Karachi convention used in Pakistan. The “Today” card above always shows the current day's times, calculated live in your browser.

Times here follow the University of Islamic Sciences, Karachi convention (Fajr 18°, Isha 18°) — the long-established standard for Pakistan and the wider subcontinent, used by the vast majority of printed timetables in Lahore.

Lahore keeps Asia/Karachi time (UTC+05:00), with no daylight-saving changes in 2026. SalahTime computes against the IANA timezone database, so any clock change is applied automatically — the times here always match the local clock in Lahore.
