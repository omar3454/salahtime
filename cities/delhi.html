About prayer times in Delhi

Prayer times in Delhi, India, shift a little every day as the sun's path changes through the year. Muslims here observe five daily prayers — Fajr before dawn, Dhuhr after midday, Asr in the afternoon, Maghrib just after sunset, and Isha at night — and this page gives the exact time of each, for every day of 2026. SalahTime computes them for Delhi (28.7041, 77.1025) with precise solar-position mathematics, following the University of Islamic Sciences, Karachi convention used in India. The “Today” card above always shows the current day's times, calculated live in your browser.

Times here follow the University of Islamic Sciences, Karachi convention (Fajr 18°, Isha 18°) — the long-established standard for India and the wider subcontinent, used by the vast majority of printed timetables in Delhi.

Delhi keeps Asia/Kolkata time (UTC+05:30), with no daylight-saving changes in 2026. SalahTime computes against the IANA timezone database, so any clock change is applied automatically — the times here always match the local clock in Delhi.

SalahTime uses the University of Islamic Sciences, Karachi — Fajr 18°, Isha 18°. This is the standard convention in India — compare it with the other ten methods using the selector above the timetable.

What time is Fajr today in Delhi?

Fajr shifts a minute or two each day; the exact time is in the “Today” card at the top of this page, computed live for the current date. Imsak (the start of the fast) falls about ten minutes before Fajr.
