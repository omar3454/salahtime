About prayer times in Yogyakarta

Prayer times in Yogyakarta, Indonesia, shift a little every day as the sun's path changes through the year. Muslims here observe five daily prayers — Fajr before dawn, Dhuhr after midday, Asr in the afternoon, Maghrib just after sunset, and Isha at night — and this page gives the exact time of each, for every day of 2026. SalahTime computes them for Yogyakarta (-7.7956, 110.3695) with precise solar-position mathematics, following the Kementerian Agama Indonesia convention used in Indonesia. The “Today” card above always shows the current day's times, calculated live in your browser.

This timetable uses the convention of Indonesia's Ministry of Religious Affairs, Kemenag (Fajr 20°, Isha 18°) — the official standard behind mosque schedules across Yogyakarta and the archipelago.

Yogyakarta keeps Asia/Jakarta time (UTC+07:00), with no daylight-saving changes in 2026. SalahTime computes against the IANA timezone database, so any clock change is applied automatically — the times here always match the local clock in Yogyakarta.
