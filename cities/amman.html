About prayer times in Amman

Prayer times in Amman, Jordan, shift a little every day as the sun's path changes through the year. Muslims here observe five daily prayers — Fajr before dawn, Dhuhr after midday, Asr in the afternoon, Maghrib just after sunset, and Isha at night — and this page gives the exact time of each, for every day of 2026. SalahTime computes them for Amman (31.9539, 35.9106) with precise solar-position mathematics, following the Ministry of Awqaf, Jordan convention used in Jordan. The “Today” card above always shows the current day's times, calculated live in your browser.

Prayer times here follow the convention of Jordan's Ministry of Awqaf (Fajr 18°, Isha 18°, Maghrib five minutes after sunset) — the official standard announced by mosques across Amman.

Amman keeps Asia/Amman time (UTC+03:00), with no daylight-saving changes in 2026. SalahTime computes against the IANA timezone database, so any clock change is applied automatically — the times here always match the local clock in Amman.
