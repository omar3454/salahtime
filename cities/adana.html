About prayer times in Adana

Prayer times in Adana, Turkey, shift a little every day as the sun's path changes through the year. Muslims here observe five daily prayers — Fajr before dawn, Dhuhr after midday, Asr in the afternoon, Maghrib just after sunset, and Isha at night — and this page gives the exact time of each, for every day of 2026. SalahTime computes them for Adana (37, 35.3213) with precise solar-position mathematics, following the Diyanet, Turkey convention used in Turkey. The “Today” card above always shows the current day's times, calculated live in your browser.

This page uses the Diyanet convention of Turkey's Directorate of Religious Affairs (Fajr 18°, Isha 17°, with small precautionary minute adjustments) — the official standard behind every mosque timetable in Adana.

Adana keeps Europe/Istanbul time (UTC+03:00), with no daylight-saving changes in 2026. SalahTime computes against the IANA timezone database, so any clock change is applied automatically — the times here always match the local clock in Adana.
