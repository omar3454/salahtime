About prayer times in Cairo

Prayer times in Cairo, Egypt, shift a little every day as the sun's path changes through the year. Muslims here observe five daily prayers — Fajr before dawn, Dhuhr after midday, Asr in the afternoon, Maghrib just after sunset, and Isha at night — and this page gives the exact time of each, for every day of 2026. SalahTime computes them for Cairo (30.0444, 31.2357) with precise solar-position mathematics, following the Egyptian General Authority of Survey convention used in Egypt. The “Today” card above always shows the current day's times, calculated live in your browser.

This page uses the Egyptian General Authority of Survey convention (19.5° for Fajr, 17.5° for Isha) — the official standard across Egypt, and the one behind mosque timetables throughout Cairo. If you learned prayer times from a local mosque calendar, they will match this page to the minute in almost every case.

Cairo keeps Africa/Cairo time (UTC+02:00) and observes daylight saving in 2026, shifting to UTC+03:00 in the warmer months. SalahTime computes against the IANA timezone database, so any clock change is applied automatically — the times here always match the local clock in Cairo.

Frequently asked questions

What calculation method is used for prayer times in Cairo?

SalahTime uses the Egyptian General Authority of Survey — Fajr 19.5°, Isha 17.5°. This is the standard convention in Egypt — compare it with the other ten methods using the selector above the timetable.

What time is Fajr today in Cairo?

Fajr shifts a minute or two each day; the exact time is in the “Today” card at the top of this page, computed live for the current date. Imsak (the start of the fast) falls about ten minutes before Fajr.

How are the prayer times on this page calculated?

From the sun's declination and the equation of time at Cairo's coordinates, applying the twilight angles of the chosen method (Fajr 19.5°, Isha at 17.5°). The engine has been verified against the Aladhan prayer-times API across multiple cities and dates.

Can I switch the calculation method?

Yes — the dropdown above the timetable switches between all eleven major methods, with a Standard/Hanafi Asr toggle. The timetable and the “Today” card recompute instantly in your browser.
