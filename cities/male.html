About prayer times in Male

Prayer times in Male, Maldives, shift a little every day as the sun's path changes through the year. Muslims here observe five daily prayers — Fajr before dawn, Dhuhr after midday, Asr in the afternoon, Maghrib just after sunset, and Isha at night — and this page gives the exact time of each, for every day of 2026. SalahTime computes them for Male (4.1755, 73.5089) with precise solar-position mathematics, following the Muslim World League convention used in Maldives. The “Today” card above always shows the current day's times, calculated live in your browser.

This timetable uses the Muslim World League convention (Fajr 18°, Isha 17°) — one of the most widely adopted standards in the world, and the common reference across the Gulf, Europe and much of Maldives's printed calendars.

Male keeps Indian/Maldives time (UTC+05:00), with no daylight-saving changes in 2026. SalahTime computes against the IANA timezone database, so any clock change is applied automatically — the times here always match the local clock in Male.

Frequently asked questions

What calculation method is used for prayer times in Male?

SalahTime uses the Muslim World League — Fajr 18°, Isha 17°. This is the standard convention in Maldives — compare it with the other ten methods using the selector above the timetable.

What time is Fajr today in Male?

Fajr shifts a minute or two each day; the exact time is in the “Today” card at the top of this page, computed live for the current date. Imsak (the start of the fast) falls about ten minutes before Fajr.

How are the prayer times on this page calculated?

From the sun's declination and the equation of time at Male's coordinates, applying the twilight angles of the chosen method (Fajr 18°, Isha at 17°). The engine has been verified against the Aladhan prayer-times API across multiple cities and dates.
