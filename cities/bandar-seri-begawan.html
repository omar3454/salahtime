About prayer times in Bandar Seri Begawan

Prayer times in Bandar Seri Begawan, Brunei, shift a little every day as the sun's path changes through the year. Muslims here observe five daily prayers — Fajr before dawn, Dhuhr after midday, Asr in the afternoon, Maghrib just after sunset, and Isha at night — and this page gives the exact time of each, for every day of 2026.
