About prayer times in Semarang

Prayer times in Semarang, Indonesia, shift a little every day as the sun's path changes through the year. Muslims here observe five daily prayers — Fajr before dawn, Dhuhr after midday, Asr in the afternoon, Maghrib just after sunset, and Isha at night — and this page gives the exact time of each, for every day of 2026. SalahTime computes them for Semarang (-6.9667, 110.4167) with precise solar-position mathematics, following the Kementerian Agama Indonesia convention used in Indonesia. The “Today” card above always shows the current day's times, calculated live in your browser.

This timetable uses the convention of Indonesia's Ministry of Religious Affairs, Kemenag (Fajr 20°, Isha 18°) — the official standard behind mosque schedules across Semarang and the archipelago.

Semarang keeps Asia/Jakarta time (UTC+07:00), with no daylight-saving changes in 2026. SalahTime computes against the IANA timezone database, so any clock change is applied automatically — the times here always match the local clock in Semarang.

Frequently asked questions

What calculation method is used for prayer times in Semarang?

SalahTime uses the Kementerian Agama Indonesia — Fajr 20°, Isha 18°. This is the standard convention in Indonesia — compare it with the other ten methods using the selector above the timetable.

What time is Fajr today in Semarang?

Fajr shifts a minute or two each day; the exact time is in the “Today” card at the top of this page, computed live for the current date. Imsak (the start of the fast) falls about ten minutes before Fajr.

How are the prayer times on this page calculated?
