About prayer times in Penang

Prayer times in Penang, Malaysia, shift a little every day as the sun's path changes through the year. Muslims here observe five daily prayers — Fajr before dawn, Dhuhr after midday, Asr in the afternoon, Maghrib just after sunset, and Isha at night — and this page gives the exact time of each, for every day of 2026. SalahTime computes them for Penang (5.4141, 100.3288) with precise solar-position mathematics, following the Jabatan Kemajuan Islam Malaysia (JAKIM) convention used in Malaysia. The “Today” card above always shows the current day's times, calculated live in your browser.

Prayer times here follow the JAKIM convention (Fajr 20°, Isha 18°) set by Malaysia's Department of Islamic Development — the official standard used by mosques throughout Penang.

Penang keeps Asia/Kuala Lumpur time (UTC+08:00), with no daylight-saving changes in 2026. SalahTime computes against the IANA timezone database, so any clock change is applied automatically — the times here always match the local clock in Penang.

Frequently asked questions

What calculation method is used for prayer times in Penang?

SalahTime uses the Jabatan Kemajuan Islam Malaysia (JAKIM) — Fajr 20°, Isha 18°. This is the standard convention in Malaysia — compare it with the other ten methods using the selector above the timetable.

What time is Fajr today in Penang?

Fajr shifts a minute or two each day; the exact time is in the “Today” card at the top of this page, computed live for the current date. Imsak (the start of the fast) falls about ten minutes before Fajr.

How are the prayer times on this page calculated?

From the sun's declination and the equation of time at Penang's coordinates, applying the twilight angles of the chosen method (Fajr 20°, Isha at 18°). The engine has been verified against the Aladhan prayer-times API across multiple cities and dates.

Can I switch the calculation method?

Yes — the dropdown above the timetable switches between all eleven major methods, with a Standard/Hanafi Asr toggle. The timetable and the “Today” card recompute instantly in your browser.
