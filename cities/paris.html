About prayer times in Paris

Prayer times in Paris, France, shift a little every day as the sun's path changes through the year. Muslims here observe five daily prayers — Fajr before dawn, Dhuhr after midday, Asr in the afternoon, Maghrib just after sunset, and Isha at night — and this page gives the exact time of each, for every day of 2026. SalahTime computes them for Paris (48.8566, 2.3522) with precise solar-position mathematics, following the UOIF, France convention used in France. The “Today” card above always shows the current day's times, calculated live in your browser.

This timetable uses the UOIF convention (Fajr 12°, Isha 12°) — the standard adopted by the Union of Islamic Organisations of France for France, with twilight angles suited to higher latitudes.

Paris keeps Europe/Paris time (UTC+01:00) and observes daylight saving in 2026, shifting to UTC+02:00 in the warmer months. SalahTime computes against the IANA timezone database, so any clock change is applied automatically — the times here always match the local clock in Paris.

Frequently asked questions

What calculation method is used for prayer times in Paris?

SalahTime uses the UOIF, France — Fajr 12°, Isha 12°. This is the standard convention in France — compare it with the other ten methods using the selector above the timetable.

What time is Fajr today in Paris?

Fajr shifts a minute or two each day; the exact time is in the “Today” card at the top of this page, computed live for the current date. Imsak (the start of the fast) falls about ten minutes before Fajr.

How are the prayer times on this page calculated?

From the sun's declination and the equation of time at Paris's coordinates, applying the twilight angles of the chosen method (Fajr 12°, Isha at 12°). The engine has been verified against the Aladhan prayer-times API across multiple cities and dates.
