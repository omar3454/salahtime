About prayer times in Jakarta

Prayer times in Jakarta, Indonesia, shift a little every day as the sun's path changes through the year. Muslims here observe five daily prayers — Fajr before dawn, Dhuhr after midday, Asr in the afternoon, Maghrib just after sunset, and Isha at night — and this page gives the exact time of each, for every day of 2026. SalahTime computes them for Jakarta (-6.2088, 106.8456) with precise solar-position mathematics, following the Kementerian Agama Indonesia convention used in Indonesia. The “Today” card above always shows the current day's times, calculated live in your browser.

This timetable uses the convention of Indonesia's Ministry of Religious Affairs, Kemenag (Fajr 20°, Isha 18°) — the official standard behind mosque schedules across Jakarta and the archipelago.

Jakarta keeps Asia/Jakarta time (UTC+07:00), with no daylight-saving changes in 2026. SalahTime computes against the IANA timezone database, so any clock change is applied automatically — the times here always match the local clock in Jakarta.

Frequently asked questions

What calculation method is used for prayer times in Jakarta?

SalahTime uses the Kementerian Agama Indonesia — Fajr 20°, Isha 18°. This is the standard convention in Indonesia — compare it with the other ten methods using the selector above the timetable.

What time is Fajr today in Jakarta?

Fajr shifts a minute or two each day; the exact time is in the “Today” card at the top of this page, computed live for the current date. Imsak (the start of the fast) falls about ten minutes before Fajr.

How are the prayer times on this page calculated?

From the sun's declination and the equation of time at Jakarta's coordinates, applying the twilight angles of the chosen method (Fajr 20°, Isha at 18°). The engine has been verified against the Aladhan prayer-times API across multiple cities and dates.

Can I switch the calculation method?

Yes — the dropdown above the timetable switches between all eleven major methods, with a Standard/Hanafi Asr toggle. The timetable and the “Today” card recompute instantly in your browser.
