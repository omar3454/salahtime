About prayer times in Fes

Prayer times in Fes, Morocco, shift a little every day as the sun's path changes through the year. Muslims here observe five daily prayers — Fajr before dawn, Dhuhr after midday, Asr in the afternoon, Maghrib just after sunset, and Isha at night — and this page gives the exact time of each, for every day of 2026. SalahTime computes them for Fes (34.0181, -5.0078) with precise solar-position mathematics, following the Ministry of Habous, Morocco convention used in Morocco. The “Today” card above always shows the current day's times, calculated live in your browser.

This page uses the convention of Morocco's Ministry of Habous (Fajr 19°, Isha 17°) — the official standard behind mosque timetables throughout Fes.

Fes keeps Africa/Casablanca time (UTC+01:00), with no daylight-saving changes in 2026. SalahTime computes against the IANA timezone database, so any clock change is applied automatically — the times here always match the local clock in Fes.
