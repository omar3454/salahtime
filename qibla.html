Tip: if the dial jumps around, wave your phone in a figure-8 to calibrate the sensor, and keep metal objects and magnets away.

No compass sensor? Do this instead

See the line to Mecca on a map

  1. Find north with any compass app, the sun (rises in the east, sets in the west), or a landmark you know.
  2. Hold the phone so the N on the dial points north.
  3. Face the direction of the Kaaba marker — that is your Qibla.

Tip: in-app browsers (opened inside other apps) block the compass sensor. Open this page in Safari or Chrome for the live compass.
