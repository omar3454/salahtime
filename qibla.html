Qibla finder

The Qibla is the direction of the Kaaba in Mecca that Muslims face during prayer. Choose your city or use your location to get the exact bearing.

—

Cairo, Egypt

The gold arrow points toward the Qibla (north is up).

Live compass

Point your phone and the arrow will track the Qibla as you turn. This uses your device's orientation sensor.

Checking for a compass sensor…

No compass sensor? Do this instead

  1. Find north with any compass app, the sun (rises in the east, sets in the west), or a landmark you know.
  2. Turn so that north is behind the top of the compass above.
  3. Face the direction of the gold arrow — that is your Qibla.
  4. Keep metal objects and magnets away from the phone for an accurate reading.
