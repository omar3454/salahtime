Privacy policy

No accounts. No signups. No tracking cookies of our own. SalahTime does not collect, store or sell your personal data. Prayer times are calculated entirely in your browser, and any preferences you set (such as your chosen city or calculation method) are stored only on your own device via local storage — they are never sent to us.

Advertising cookies

This site is supported by advertising. Our advertising partner, Google AdSense, may set cookies on your device to serve and measure ads. You can learn how Google uses cookies and manage your choices in Google's cookie policy and Google's advertising policy.

Your rights

Under the GDPR and the CCPA/CPRA you have the right to access, delete and opt out of the sale or sharing of your personal information. Because we do not collect personal information ourselves, there is nothing to request from us; for data held by our advertising partner, please use Google's privacy controls linked above.
