Waking Up & Before Sleep

What to say when you wake up, when you lie down, and in the night.

  • After a Bad Dream

    A bad dream is from Satan, so you turn to Allah and refuse to fear it.

    Source: Sahih Muslim 2261

  • Lying Down to Sleep

    You hand your soul to Allah as you sleep. Asking for mercy if you don't wake, and protection if you do.

    Source: Sahih al-Bukhari 6320; Sahih Muslim 2714

  • The Last Two Verses of Al-Baqarah

    Two verses that gather the whole religion: belief, obedience, then the most tender pleas. Don't blame us when we forget, don't burden us beyond our strength. Pardon us, forgive us, have mercy on us.

    Source: Qur'an 2:285-286

  • When Frightened at Night

    Allah's perfect words are a shield around you when the night feels frightening.

    Source: Sunan al-Tirmidhi 3528

  • In Your Name I Live and Die

    The shortest bedtime dua: your sleep and your waking are both in Allah's name. Nothing more needed.

    Source: Sahih al-Bukhari 6325

  • Protection While You Sleep

    Short words that put the Last Day before your eyes as you close them, and ask Allah's protection from its punishment.

    Source: Sunan Abi Dawud 5045

  • Opening the Night Prayer

    The Prophet opened his night prayer by standing before Allah with praise and complete surrender.

    Source: Sahih al-Bukhari 1120

  • Waking Up at Night

    Waking words that turn a restless night into a moment of answered dua.

    Source: Sahih al-Bukhari 1154
