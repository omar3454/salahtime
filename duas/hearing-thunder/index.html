Daily Life

Hearing Thunder

Source: Muwatta Malik

سُبْحَانَ الَّذِي يُسَبِّحُ الرَّعْدُ بِحَمْدِهِ وَالْمَلَائِكَةُ مِنْ خِيفَتِهِ

Transliteration

Subhanalladhi yusabbihur-ra'du bihamdihi wal-mala'ikatu min khifatih

Translation

Glory be to the One whom the thunder glorifies with His praise, and the angels out of fear of Him.

What this dua means

It echoes the Qur'an's words about thunder glorifying Allah.

When to read it

Say it when you hear thunder.

Its virtue

It was the practice of Abdullah ibn al-Zubayr, who would stop speaking and say it.
