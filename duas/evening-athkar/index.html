Evening Athkar

The complete evening remembrances. Read them after Asr, as the day winds down. Tap any one for its Arabic text, meaning, and virtue.

  • 1. Ayat al-Kursi

    Whoever recites it in the morning is protected until the evening.

    Repeat 1x · Source: Qur'an 2:255

  • 2. The Last Three Surahs

    Reciting these three surahs three times in the morning suffices as protection for the day.

    Repeat 3x each · Source: Abu Dawud 5082, Tirmidhi 3575

  • 3. We Have Reached the Evening

    The Prophet's ﷺ own evening supplication. Closing the day under Allah's care.

    Repeat 1x · Source: Sahih Muslim 2723

  • 4. O Allah, By You We Reach the Evening

    Ending the day as it began. Everything by Allah, returning to Allah.

    Repeat 1x · Source: Tirmidhi 3391

  • 5. Sayyid al-Istighfar (Best of Seeking Forgiveness)

    Whoever says it in the morning with certainty and dies that day enters Paradise.

    Repeat 1x · Source: Sahih al-Bukhari 6306

  • 6. I Am Pleased with Allah as Lord

    Allah has promised Paradise to whoever says this with sincerity.

    Repeat 3x · Source: Abu Dawud 5072

  • 7. O Allah, Grant Me Health

    A morning request for the three blessings we use every day: body, hearing, sight.

    Repeat 3x · Source: Abu Dawud 5090

  • 8. Refuge from Disbelief and Poverty

    Protection for both worlds. Faith and provision in this life, safety in the next.

    Repeat 3x · Source: Abu Dawud 5090

  • 9. Allah Is Sufficient for Me

    Whoever says it seven times morning and evening, Allah suffices him against whatever worries him.

    Repeat 7x · Source: Abu Dawud 5081

  • 10. In Allah's Name, Nothing Harms

    No sudden affliction befalls whoever says it three times in the morning.

    Repeat 3x · Source: Tirmidhi 3388, Abu Dawud 5088

  • 11. Refuge in Allah's Perfect Words

    A shield against every created harm, said morning and evening.

    Repeat 3x · Source: Sahih Muslim 2709

  • 12. Glory and Praise to Allah (100x)

    Whoever says it 100 times a day has his sins wiped away, even if like the foam of the sea.

    Repeat 100x · Source: Sahih al-Bukhari 6405, Sahih Muslim 2691

  • 13. The Declaration of Oneness (100x)

    100 times = reward of freeing 10 slaves, 100 good deeds written, 100 sins erased, and a shield from Satan all day.

    Repeat 100x · Source: Sahih al-Bukhari 6403

  • 14. Glorification Beyond Number

    The Prophet ﷺ said these words outweigh a whole morning of worship.

    Repeat 3x · Source: Sahih Muslim 2726

  • 15. Asking for Pardon and Wellbeing

    The most comprehensive protection dua, covering faith, family, wealth, and all six directions.

    Repeat 1x · Source: Ibn Majah 3871, Abu Dawud 5074

  • 16. Knower of the Unseen

    The Prophet ﷺ taught this to Abu Bakr. Protection from one's own soul and from Satan.

    Repeat 1x · Source: Tirmidhi 3392, Abu Dawud 5067

  • 17. O Living, O Sustainer

    The Prophet's ﷺ counsel to Fatimah. Total reliance on Allah's mercy for every affair.

    Repeat 1x · Source: Tirmidhi 3524 (hasan)

  • 18. We Reach the Evening on the Fitrah of Islam

    An evening declaration of identity. Ending the day anchored in faith.

    Repeat 1x · Source: Ahmad 15367
