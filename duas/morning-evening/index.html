Duaa Library

Morning & Evening

Daily remembrances for the start and end of your day.

  • O Allah, Grant Me Health

    You ask Allah for health in your body, your hearing, and your sight, repeating it three times.

    Source: Abu Dawud 5090

  • Asking for Afiyah Morning and Evening

    Afiyah means complete safety and well-being of body, faith, and life.

    Source: Sunan Abi Dawud 5074

  • Asking for Pardon and Wellbeing

    You ask Allah for pardon and complete wellbeing: in your faith, your life, your family, and your wealth.

    Source: Ibn Majah 3871, Abu Dawud 5074

  • Knower of the Unseen

    You call on Allah, Knower of the unseen and the seen, and ask His protection through the coming hours.

    Source: Tirmidhi 3392, Abu Dawud 5067

  • We Reach the Evening on the Fitrah of Islam

    You enter the night declaring the fitrah: Islam, the word of sincerity, and the way of Ibrahim.

    Source: Ahmad 15367

  • We Wake on the Fitrah of Islam

    You wake declaring the fitrah: Islam, the word of sincerity, and the way of Ibrahim.

    Source: Ahmad 15367

  • Refuge in Allah's Perfect Words

    You seek refuge in Allah's perfect words from the evil of everything He created.

    Source: Sahih Muslim 2709

  • Refuge from Disbelief and Poverty

    A threefold plea for refuge: from disbelief, from poverty, and from the punishment of the grave.

    Source: Abu Dawud 5090

  • O Allah, By You We Reach the Evening

    You place the whole night in Allah's hands: by Him you reach the evening, by Him you live, and to Him is the return.

    Source: Tirmidhi 3391

  • O Allah, By You We Reach the Morning

    You place the whole day in Allah's hands: by Him you wake, by Him you live, and to Him is the return.

    Source: Tirmidhi 3391

  • Declaration of Contentment

    Three declarations of loyalty: your Lord, your way of life, your Prophet. Saying it renews your covenant every day.

    Source: Sunan Abi Dawud 5072

  • Evening Remembrance

    The evening twin of the morning dhikr. As night falls, you hand the night to Allah, asking for its good and refuge from its harm.

    Source: Sahih Muslim 2723

  • Glory and Praise, a Hundred Times

    Two words that declare Allah perfect above all flaws, and praise Him at the same time. Light on the tongue, heavy on the scale.

    Source: Sahih al-Bukhari 6405; Sahih Muslim 2691

  • Allah Is Sufficient for Me

    Seven times you declare that Allah is enough for you, the best disposer of all affairs.

    Source: Abu Dawud 5081

  • The Last Three Surahs

    Al-Ikhlas, Al-Falaq, and An-Nas. Three short chapters that gather the heart of seeking protection, each read three times.

    Source: Abu Dawud 5082, Tirmidhi 3575

  • Morning Gratitude

    It starts the day by tracing every blessing back to Allah alone.

    Source: Sunan Abi Dawud 5073

  • Morning Remembrance

    A morning reset. You declare that the new day, and everything in it, belongs to Allah. Praise Him, then ask for the day's good and protection from its harm.

    Source: Sahih Muslim 2723

  • Protection, Three Times

    You begin in Allah's name, declaring that nothing can harm you while you are under His name. He hears and knows everything.

    Source: Sunan Abi Dawud 5088; Jami' at-Tirmidhi 3388

  • Sayyid al-Istighfar: The Master of Seeking Forgiveness

    Called 'the master of seeking forgiveness' because it gathers everything: Allah's lordship, your servitude, admission of His blessings and your sins. Then the plea.

    Source: Sahih al-Bukhari 6306

  • Glorification Beyond Number

    Glorification beyond counting: by the number of His creation, His pleasure, the weight of His throne, and the ink of His words.

    Source: Sahih Muslim 2726

  • The Declaration of Oneness, a Hundred Times

    The core testimony of Islam, repeated: Allah alone deserves worship; all power and praise belong to Him.

    Source: Sahih al-Bukhari 3293; Sahih Muslim 2691

  • When You Wake Up

    Sleep is the little death; waking is a little resurrection. The first words of your day are gratitude for another chance.

    Source: Sahih al-Bukhari 6312
