Duaa Library

Daily Life

For eating, home, the mosque, travel, and the small moments in between.

  • Seeing Someone Afflicted

    It thanks Allah for sparing you while keeping mercy in your heart for the afflicted.

    Source: Sunan al-Tirmidhi 3431

  • After the Azan

    It asks Allah to honor the Prophet with the highest rank in Paradise.

    Source: Sahih al-Bukhari 614

  • After Eating

    It thanks Allah for the food and admits the meal came from Him, not from your own effort.

    Source: Sunan al-Tirmidhi 3458

  • After the Rain

    It gives the credit for rain to Allah's grace, not to the stars or the seasons.

    Source: Sahih al-Bukhari 846

  • After Wudu

    It renews the testimony of faith right after purifying the body.

    Source: Sahih Muslim 234

  • Before Eating

    It puts the meal under Allah's name and blessing.

    Source: Sahih al-Bukhari 5376

  • Before Wudu

    It begins the ablution with Allah's name, as every act of worship should begin.

    Source: Sunan Abi Dawud 101

  • Entering the Bathroom

    It asks Allah for protection in a place where one should not remember Allah aloud.

    Source: Sahih al-Bukhari 142

  • Entering the Home

    It marks every coming and going with Allah's name and trust in Him.

    Source: Sahih Muslim 2018

  • Entering the Market

    It fills a busy, heedless place with the remembrance of Allah.

    Source: Sunan al-Tirmidhi 3428

  • Entering the Mosque

    It seeks Allah's protection the moment you step into His house.

    Source: Sunan Abi Dawud 466

  • Getting Dressed

    It thanks Allah for the clothes you wear, the same way you thank Him for your food.

    Source: Sunan Abi Dawud 4023

  • Hearing Thunder

    It echoes the Qur'an's words about thunder glorifying Allah.

    Source: Muwatta Malik

  • Leaving the Bathroom

    It asks Allah's pardon for the moments spent without His remembrance.

    Source: Sunan Abi Dawud 30

  • When Leaving the House

    Three sentences at your doorstep: Allah's name, your trust in Him, and the admission that all strength is His.

    Source: Sunan Abi Dawud 5095; Jami' at-Tirmidhi 3426

  • Leaving the Mosque

    It sends salawat on the Prophet and asks Allah for provision as you return to your day.

    Source: Sahih Muslim 713

  • When Sneezing

    The sneezer praises Allah for the blessing, and those who hear it pray for his mercy.

    Source: Sahih al-Bukhari 6224

  • The Traveler's Dua

    You start by glorifying Allah above the vehicle that carries you. Then ask Him for an easy, blessed journey, and leave your family in His care.

    Source: Sahih Muslim 1342

  • When Angry

    Anger comes from Shaytan, and seeking refuge cuts off his influence.

    Source: Sahih al-Bukhari 6115

  • When It Rains

    It asks Allah that the rain brings benefit, not harm.

    Source: Sahih al-Bukhari 1032

  • When the Wind Blows

    It asks for the benefit of the wind and protection from any harm it carries.

    Source: Sahih Muslim 899
