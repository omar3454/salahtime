After the Obligatory Prayers

You Are Peace

Source: Sahih Muslim 592

اللَّهُمَّ أَنْتَ السَّلَامُ وَمِنْكَ السَّلَامُ، تَبَارَكْتَ يَا ذَا الْجَلَالِ وَالْإِكْرَامِ

Transliteration

Allahumma antas-salam, wa minkas-salam, tabarakta ya dhal-jalali wal-ikram.

Translation

O Allah, You are Peace, and from You comes peace. Blessed are You, O Owner of Majesty and Honor.

What this dua means

You turn to Allah after prayer, asking Him to bless your worship and keep it free of flaw.

When to read it

Say it after the three istighfars following each obligatory prayer.

Its virtue

It was the Prophet's habit after every prayer, opening the door to more remembrance.
