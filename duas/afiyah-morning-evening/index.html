Asking for Afiyah Morning and Evening

Source: Sunan Abi Dawud 5074

اللَّهُمَّ إِنِّي أَسْأَلُكَ الْعَافِيَةَ فِي الدُّنْيَا وَالْآخِرَةِ

Transliteration

Allahumma inni as'aluka al-'afiyah fid-dunya wal-akhirah

Translation

O Allah, I ask You for well-being in this world and the Hereafter.

What this dua means

Afiyah means complete safety and well-being of body, faith, and life.

When to read it

Say it every morning and every evening.

Its virtue

The Prophet said no one is given anything better after certainty of faith than well-being.
