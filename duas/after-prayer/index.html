After the Obligatory Prayers

Duas to say after the five daily prayers.

  • You Are Peace

    You turn to Allah after prayer, asking Him to bless your worship and keep it free of flaw.

    Source: Sahih Muslim 592

  • Ayat al-Kursi

    The greatest verse of the Quran. A complete portrait of Allah: eternally alive, sustaining all, all-knowing, all-powerful.

    Source: Qur'an 2:255

  • The Most Comprehensive Dua

    Good in this world, good in the next, and safety from the Fire. Everything a human could want, in one line.

    Source: Qur'an 2:201

  • Help Me Remember You

    Worship needs help, even remembrance. You ask Allah for assistance in the three pillars: remembering Him, thanking Him, worshipping Him well.

    Source: Sunan Abi Dawud 1522

  • The Three Protector Surahs

    These short surahs wrap you in Allah's protection.

    Source: Sunan Abi Dawud 1523

  • Make Me Steadfast in Prayer

    Prophet Ibrahim's dua: make prayer the anchor of my life, and of my children's lives after me.

    Source: Qur'an 14:40

  • Refuge from Four

    The four greatest fears, covered in one dua. Said in the final sitting of every prayer, right before the salam.

    Source: Sahih al-Bukhari 1377; Sahih Muslim 588

  • Seeking Forgiveness After Prayer

    You begin with forgiveness because even your prayer has shortcomings.

    Source: Sahih Muslim 591

  • Tasbih After Prayer

    This simple counting fills the moments after prayer with glorification and praise.

    Source: Sahih al-Bukhari 843
