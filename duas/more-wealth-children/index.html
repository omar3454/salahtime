Dua for Blessed Provision

Source: Sahih al-Bukhari 6334

اللَّهُمَّ أَكْثِرْ مَالَهُ وَوَلَدَهُ، وَبَارِكْ لَهُ فِيمَا أَعْطَيْتَهُ

Transliteration

Allahumma akthir malahu wa waladahu, wa barik lahu fima a'taytah.

Translation

O Allah, increase his wealth and his children, and bless for him what You have given him.

What this dua means

The Prophet's own dua for his young servant Anas, asking for plenty with blessing.

When to read it

Make it for your children or anyone you wish prosperity for.

Its virtue

Anas lived to see its answer in a long life, many children, and abundant provision.
