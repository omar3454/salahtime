Provision & Blessings

Asking Allah for lawful provision, gratitude, and blessing.

  • Beneficial Knowledge and Good Provision

    The three things a believer's day should produce: knowledge that helps, earnings that are pure, and deeds Allah accepts.

    Source: Sunan Ibn Majah 925

  • Guidance, Piety, Chastity, Contentment

    Four treasures in one line: the right direction, God-consciousness, dignity, and a heart rich enough to need nothing.

    Source: Sahih Muslim 2721

  • Sufficiency Through the Halal

    You ask Allah to make the halal enough for you so you never need the haram, and to enrich you so you never need anyone but Him.

    Source: Jami' at-Tirmidhi 3563

  • Increase Me in Knowledge

    Three words, among the shortest duas in the Quran, for students of every age. The knowledge meant here is the kind that brings you closer to Allah.

    Source: Qur'an 20:114

  • The Complete Thank You

    The fullest way to thank someone, because you ask Allah Himself to reward them.

    Source: Sunan al-Tirmidhi 2035

  • The Heavenly Table

    A Qur'anic prayer asking Allah for provision and blessing from above.

    Source: Qur'an 5:114

  • Dua for Blessed Provision

    The Prophet's own dua for his young servant Anas, asking for plenty with blessing.

    Source: Sahih al-Bukhari 6334

  • Asking for Wholesome Provision

    A morning request for the three things that make a day worthwhile.

    Source: Sunan Ibn Majah 925

  • Protect My Blessings

    Blessings can vanish overnight. This dua asks Allah to keep what He gave you: health, provision, safety. And to keep His anger away.

    Source: Sahih Muslim 2739

  • Righteous Family

    You ask for the greatest provision: a family that is a joy to look at. Then aim higher still: to lead the righteous.

    Source: Qur'an 25:74

  • Teach Me Gratitude

    Prophet Sulaiman's dua: gratitude isn't automatic. You ask Allah to inspire it in you, for your blessings and your parents'.

    Source: Qur'an 27:19

  • Refuge from Useless Knowledge

    It asks Allah to keep your knowledge useful, your heart soft, and your duas alive.

    Source: Sahih Muslim 2722
