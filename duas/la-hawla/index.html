Hardship, Anxiety & Sadness

Treasure of Paradise

Source: Sahih al-Bukhari 6384

لَا حَوْلَ وَلَا قُوَّةَ إِلَّا بِاللهِ

Transliteration

La hawla wa la quwwata illa billah.

Translation

There is no power and no strength except with Allah.

What this dua means

Every strength you have comes from Allah alone.

When to read it

Say it often, in ease and in difficulty alike.

Its virtue

The Prophet called it one of the treasures of Paradise.
