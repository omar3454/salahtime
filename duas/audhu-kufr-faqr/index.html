Morning & Evening

Refuge from Disbelief and Poverty

Source: Abu Dawud 5090

اللَّهُمَّ إِنِّي أَعُوذُ بِكَ مِنَ الْكُفْرِ وَالْفَقْرِ، وَأَعُوذُ بِكَ مِنْ عَذَابِ الْقَبْرِ، لَا إِلَهَ إِلَّا أَنْتَ

Repeat: 3x

Transliteration

Allahumma inni a'udhu bika minal-kufri wal-faqr, wa a'udhu bika min 'adhabil-qabr, la ilaha illa ant.

Translation

O Allah, I seek refuge in You from disbelief and poverty, and I seek refuge in You from the punishment of the grave. None has the right to be worshipped but You.

What this dua means

A threefold plea for refuge: from disbelief, from poverty, and from the punishment of the grave.

When to read it

Every morning and evening.

Its virtue

Protection for both worlds. Faith and provision in this life, safety in the next.
