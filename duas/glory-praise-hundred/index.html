Glory and Praise, a Hundred Times

Source: Sahih al-Bukhari 6405; Sahih Muslim 2691

سُبْحَانَ اللَّهِ وَبِحَمْدِهِ

Transliteration

SubhanAllahi wa bihamdih.

Translation

Glory be to Allah and praise Him.

What this dua means

Two words that declare Allah perfect above all flaws — and praise Him at the same time. Light on the tongue, heavy on the scale.

When to read it

A hundred times in the morning — or any time of day.

Virtue

The Prophet ﷺ said: whoever says it a hundred times in a day, his sins are forgiven even if they are like the foam of the sea. (Bukhari 6405, Muslim 2691)
