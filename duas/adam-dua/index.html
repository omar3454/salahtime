Forgiveness & Repentance

The Dua of Adam

Source: Qur'an 7:23

رَبَّنَا ظَلَمْنَا أَنفُسَنَا وَإِن لَّمْ تَغْفِرْ لَنَا وَتَرْحَمْنَا لَنَكُونَنَّ مِنَ الْخَاسِرِينَ

Transliteration

Rabbanā ẓalamnā anfusanā, wa in lam taghfir lanā wa tarḥamnā lanakūnanna mina'l-khāsirīn.

Translation

Our Lord, we have wronged ourselves, and if You do not forgive us and have mercy upon us, we will surely be among the losers.

What this dua means

The first repentance in human history. Adam blamed no one. He said 'we wronged ourselves,' then begged forgiveness and mercy.

When to read it

After any sin, big or small. It is the model way back to Allah.

Its virtue

Allah taught Adam these very words, then forgave him (Qur'an 2:37). The same words still open the same door.
