Leaving the Mosque

Source: Sahih Muslim 713

بِسْمِ اللَّهِ، اللَّهُمَّ صَلِّ عَلَى مُحَمَّدٍ، اللَّهُمَّ إِنِّي أَسْأَلُكَ مِنْ فَضْلِكَ

Transliteration

Bismillah, Allahumma salli 'ala Muhammad, Allahumma inni as'aluka min fadlik

Translation

In the name of Allah. O Allah, send blessings upon Muhammad. O Allah, I ask You of Your bounty.

What this dua means

It sends salawat on the Prophet and asks Allah for provision as you return to your day.

When to read it

Say it with your left foot first as you leave the mosque.

Its virtue

It follows the Prophet's own practice and keeps you connected to him in the small moments.
