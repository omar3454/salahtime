Rizq (Provision)

Guidance, Piety, Chastity, Contentment

Source: Sahih Muslim 2721

اللَّهُمَّ إِنِّي أَسْأَلُكَ الْهُدَى وَالتُّقَى وَالْعَفَافَ وَالْغِنَى

Transliteration

Allahumma inni as'alukal-huda wat-tuqa wal-'afafa wal-ghina.

Translation

O Allah, I ask You for guidance, piety, chastity, and contentment.

What this dua means

Four treasures in one line: the right direction, God-consciousness, dignity, and a heart rich enough to need nothing.

When to read it

Any time — a perfect all-purpose daily dua.

Virtue

From the Prophet's ﷺ concise supplications — four words covering this world and the next. (Muslim 2721)
