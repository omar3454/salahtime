Morning & Evening

Refuge in Allah's Perfect Words

Source: Sahih Muslim 2709

أَعُوذُ بِكَلِمَاتِ اللَّهِ التَّامَّاتِ مِنْ شَرِّ مَا خَلَقَ

Repeat: 3x

Transliteration

A'udhu bikalimatillahit-tammati min sharri ma khalaq.

Translation

I seek refuge in Allah's perfect words from the evil of what He created.

What this dua means

You seek refuge in Allah's perfect words from the evil of everything He created.

When to read it

Every morning and evening.

Its virtue

A shield against every created harm, said morning and evening.
