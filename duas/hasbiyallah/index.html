Morning & Evening

Allah Is Sufficient for Me

Source: Abu Dawud 5081

حَسْبِيَ اللَّهُ لَا إِلَهَ إِلَّا هُوَ، عَلَيْهِ تَوَكَّلْتُ وَهُوَ رَبُّ الْعَرْشِ الْعَظِيمِ

Repeat: 7x

Transliteration

Hasbiyallahu la ilaha illa huwa, 'alayhi tawakkaltu wa huwa rabbul-'arshil-'azim.

Translation

Allah is sufficient for me. None has the right to be worshipped but Him. In Him I place my trust, and He is the Lord of the Mighty Throne.

What this dua means

Seven times you declare that Allah is enough for you, the best disposer of all affairs.

When to read it

Every morning and evening.

Its virtue

Whoever says it seven times morning and evening, Allah suffices him against whatever worries him.
