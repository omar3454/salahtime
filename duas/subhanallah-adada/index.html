Morning & Evening

Glorification Beyond Number

Source: Sahih Muslim 2726

سُبْحَانَ اللَّهِ وَبِحَمْدِهِ، عَدَدَ خَلْقِهِ، وَرِضَا نَفْسِهِ، وَزِنَةَ عَرْشِهِ، وَمِدَادَ كَلِمَاتِهِ

Repeat: 3x

Transliteration

Subhanallahi wa bihamdih, 'adada khalqihi, wa rida nafsihi, wa zinata 'arshihi, wa midada kalimatih.

Translation

Glory be to Allah and praise Him, as many times as His creation, as much as pleases Him, as heavy as His Throne, and as boundless as His words.

What this dua means

Glorification beyond counting: by the number of His creation, His pleasure, the weight of His throne, and the ink of His words.

When to read it

Every morning and evening.

Its virtue

The Prophet ﷺ said these words outweigh a whole morning of worship.
