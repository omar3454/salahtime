Hardship, Anxiety & Sadness

Duas for distress, worry, grief, and difficult moments.

  • Against Worry and Grief

    Eight things the Prophet ﷺ sought refuge from. A complete map of human weakness: anxiety about the future, grief over the past, and everything that paralyzes you in between.

    Source: Sahih al-Bukhari 6369

  • When Calamity Strikes

    Calamity becomes bearable when you remember everything comes from Allah and returns to Him.

    Source: Sahih Muslim 918

  • Dua of Distress

    When distress closes in, you call on Allah by His greatness and His mercy together.

    Source: Sahih al-Bukhari 6346

  • Relief from Grief

    A gentle plea for Allah's mercy to carry you when your own strength runs out.

    Source: Sunan Abi Dawud 5090

  • Allah Is Enough for Us

    A short verse that hands your worries over to Allah completely.

    Source: Qur'an 3:173

  • Treasure of Paradise

    Every strength you have comes from Allah alone.

    Source: Sahih al-Bukhari 6384

  • The Dua of Musa Before the Hard Task

    Prophet Musa's dua before facing Pharaoh, the hardest conversation of his life. He asked for an open heart, an easy task, and words people understand.

    Source: Qur'an 20:25-28

  • Dua for Patience and Strength

    The believers' dua before battle: don't just give us patience. Pour it over us like rain, and keep our feet firm so we don't run.

    Source: Qur'an 2:250

  • Keep My Heart Firm

    Hearts turn. This dua asks Allah not to let yours turn away after He guided it, and to grant you mercy from Himself, for He is the Generous Giver.

    Source: Qur'an 3:8

  • Dua for Pain

    You place your hand where it hurts and hand the pain over to Allah's power.

    Source: Sahih Muslim 2202

  • Ya Hayyu, Ya Qayyum

    You call Allah by the two names that mean He never dies and everything depends on Him. Then admit you can't manage even a blink without Him.

    Source: Jami' at-Tirmidhi 3524

  • The Dua of Yunus

    The cry of Prophet Yunus from inside the whale, in total darkness: first Allah's oneness, then His perfection, then an honest confession. Allah answered him, and He answers whoever calls with it.

    Source: Qur'an 21:87
