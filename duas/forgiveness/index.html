Forgiveness & Repentance

Turning back to Allah and seeking His pardon.

  • The Dua of Adam

    The first repentance in human history. Adam blamed no one. He said 'we wronged ourselves,' then begged forgiveness and mercy.

    Source: Qur'an 7:23

  • Forgiveness for All Sins

    It leaves no sin out: every kind, every time, every place.

    Source: Sahih Muslim 483

  • The Believers' Plea

    You come to Allah with the one thing you have, your faith, and ask Him to trade it for forgiveness and safety from the Fire.

    Source: Qur'an 3:16

  • Forgiveness for All Believers

    You ask forgiveness not only for yourself but for every believer.

    Source: Musnad Ahmad (hasan)

  • Do Not Blame Us When We Forget

    We forget. We slip. This dua asks Allah not to hold our mistakes against us. And He has promised He won't.

    Source: Qur'an 2:286 (excerpt)

  • Seeking Forgiveness Daily

    A daily habit of returning to Allah, no matter how the day went.

    Source: Sahih al-Bukhari 6307

  • Expiation of a Gathering

    It wipes clean any idle or careless words spoken during a gathering.

    Source: Sunan al-Tirmidhi 3433

  • My Lord, Forgive Me

    You call on Allah by His names of forgiveness because He loves to forgive.

    Source: Sunan Abi Dawud 1516

  • Count Me Among the Witnesses

    Faith plus following. Then the request: register me among those who testify to the truth.

    Source: Qur'an 3:53
