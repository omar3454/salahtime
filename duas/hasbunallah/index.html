Allah Is Enough for Us

Source: Qur'an 3:173

حَسْبُنَا اللهُ وَنِعْمَ الْوَكِيلُ

Transliteration

Hasbunallah wa ni'mal-wakil.

Translation

Allah is sufficient for us, and He is the best Disposer of affairs.

What this dua means

A short verse that hands your worries over to Allah completely.

When to read it

Say it when you feel afraid, threatened, or overwhelmed.

Its virtue

It was the words of the believers who turned back their enemies without any loss.
