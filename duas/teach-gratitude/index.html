Gratitude

Teach Me Gratitude

Source: Qur'an 27:19

رَبِّ أَوْزِعْنِي أَنْ أَشْكُرَ نِعْمَتَكَ الَّتِي أَنْعَمْتَ عَلَيَّ وَعَلَى وَالِدَيَّ وَأَنْ أَعْمَلَ صَالِحًا تَرْضَاهُ وَأَدْخِلْنِي بِرَحْمَتِكَ فِي عِبَادِكَ الصَّالِحِينَ

Transliteration

Rabbi awzi'ni an ashkura ni'matakallati an'amta 'alayya wa 'ala walidayy, wa an a'mala salihan tardah, wa adkhilni birahmatika fi 'ibadikas-salihin.

Translation

My Lord, inspire me to be grateful for Your favor which You bestowed upon me and upon my parents, and to do righteousness You approve of — and admit me by Your mercy among Your righteous servants.

What this dua means

Prophet Sulaiman's dua: gratitude isn't automatic — you ask Allah to inspire it in you, for your blessings and your parents'.

When to read it

When counting your blessings, and as a daily gratitude habit.

Virtue

The dua of Sulaiman (peace be upon him) at the height of his kingdom — gratitude at the peak is the hardest and noblest kind.
