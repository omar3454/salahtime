Forgiveness & Repentance

Forgiveness for All Sins

Source: Sahih Muslim 483

اللَّهُمَّ اغْفِرْ لِي ذَنْبِي كُلَّهُ، دِقَّهُ وَجِلَّهُ، وَأَوَّلَهُ وَآخِرَهُ، وَعَلَانِيَتَهُ وَسِرَّهُ

Transliteration

Allahummaghfir li dhanbi kullah, diqqahu wa jillah, wa awwalahu wa akhirah, wa 'alaniyatahu wa sirrah.

Translation

O Allah, forgive me all my sins, the small and the great, the first and the last, the open and the hidden.

What this dua means

It leaves no sin out: every kind, every time, every place.

When to read it

Say it in sujood during prayer, as the Prophet did.

Its virtue

Its completeness covers sins you remember and sins you forgot.
