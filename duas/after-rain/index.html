Daily Life

After the Rain

Source: Sahih al-Bukhari 846

مُطِرْنَا بِفَضْلِ اللَّهِ وَرَحْمَتِهِ

Transliteration

Mutirna bifadlillahi wa rahmatih

Translation

We were given rain by the bounty of Allah and His mercy.

What this dua means

It gives the credit for rain to Allah's grace, not to the stars or the seasons.

When to read it

Say it after the rain stops.

Its virtue

It declares true faith, while attributing rain to the stars is disbelief in Allah.
