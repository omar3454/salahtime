Morning & Evening

Protection, Three Times

Source: Sunan Abi Dawud 5088; Jami' at-Tirmidhi 3388

بِسْمِ اللَّهِ الَّذِي لَا يَضُرُّ مَعَ اسْمِهِ شَيْءٌ فِي الْأَرْضِ وَلَا فِي السَّمَاءِ وَهُوَ السَّمِيعُ الْعَلِيمُ

Transliteration

Bismillahilladhi la yadurru ma'asmihi shay'un fil-ardi wa la fis-sama', wa huwas-sami'ul-'alim.

Translation

In the name of Allah, with whose name nothing on earth or in heaven can cause harm. And He is the All-Hearing, the All-Knowing.

What this dua means

You begin in Allah's name, declaring that nothing can harm you while you are under His name. He hears and knows everything.

When to read it

Three times every morning and three times every evening.

Its virtue

The Prophet ﷺ said: whoever says it three times in the morning, no sudden affliction will befall him until the evening; and whoever says it in the evening, none will befall him until the morning. (Abu Dawud 5088)
