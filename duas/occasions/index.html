Duaa Library

Friday & Special Occasions

For Fridays, Ramadan, Eid, visiting the sick, and more.

  • Dua of Arafah Day

    The Prophet called this the best dua ever said, by him and all the prophets.

    Source: Sunan al-Tirmidhi 3585

  • Breaking the Fast

    It celebrates the end of the fast with gratitude and hope in Allah's reward.

    Source: Sunan Abi Dawud 2357

  • Eid Takbeer

    It fills Eid with the declaration of Allah's greatness and praise.

    Source: reported from Ibn Mas'ud

  • For One's Parents

    It asks Allah's forgiveness for yourself, your parents, and all the believers.

    Source: Qur'an 14:41

  • Salawat on Friday

    It increases blessings on the Prophet on the best day of the week.

    Source: Sunan Abi Dawud 1531

  • Dua for the Deceased

    It is the most complete prayer for the dead, asking Allah for forgiveness and Paradise.

    Source: Sahih Muslim 963

  • Visiting the Graveyard

    It greets the dead with peace and asks Allah for well-being for the living and the dead.

    Source: Sahih Muslim 975

  • Dua of Laylat al-Qadr

    It asks Allah for complete pardon on the most valuable night of the year.

    Source: Sunan al-Tirmidhi 3513

  • Sighting the New Moon

    It welcomes each new lunar month with a prayer for safety and faith.

    Source: Sunan al-Tirmidhi 3451

  • Visiting the Sick

    It asks Allah alone for complete healing for the sick person.

    Source: Sahih al-Bukhari 5675
